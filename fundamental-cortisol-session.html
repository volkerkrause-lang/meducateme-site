Prediction

Remove cortisol

Now run normal physiology backwards. Loss of glucose support and vascular responsiveness becomes dangerous during fasting, infection or trauma.

Which combination fits cortisol deficiency?

Cortisol ↓
→ gluconeogenic support ↓
→ vascular responsiveness ↓
→ hypoglycaemia + hypotension
→ severe stress can trigger adrenal crisis

Primary adrenal failure can also remove aldosterone, adding salt loss, hypovolaemia and hyperkalaemia.

Localise the failure

Primary vs central

The HPA axis predicts the laboratory pattern.

Primary adrenal failure

Cortisol ↓ → feedback ↓ → ACTH ↑. If cortex broadly affected, aldosterone may also fall. High ACTH can produce hyperpigmentation.

Central failure

ACTH drive is low/inappropriately normal → cortisol ↓. Aldosterone is usually preserved because RAAS is its major regulator.

Break the pathway

Congenital adrenal hyperplasia

CAH is easiest when derived rather than memorised: block an enzyme, lose downstream product, accumulate precursor, lose cortisol feedback, raise ACTH, enlarge the adrenal and divert substrate down open pathways.

Enzyme block → cortisol ↓ → ACTH ↑ → adrenal stimulation/hyperplasia

Precursor ↑ → shunting into pathways that remain open
Commonest defect

21-hydroxylase deficiency

Cortisol synthesis falls; severe disease also impairs aldosterone. 17-hydroxyprogesterone accumulates and steroid flux shifts toward androgen synthesis.

Clinical interruption

A 10-day-old is collapsing

Reconstruct

One system.
One causal story.

Adrenal zones determine hormones. Cholesterol supplies the substrate. ACTH drives cortisol production. Cortisol feeds back and supports fuel plus vascular tone. Break cortisol synthesis and ACTH rises. Block 21-hydroxylase and the same map predicts cortisol deficiency, androgen excess and—when severe—aldosterone deficiency.
