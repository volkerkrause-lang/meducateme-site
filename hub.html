The MeducateMe idea

DON’T JUST LEARN
WHAT HAPPENS
UNDERSTAND WHY

MeducateMe is built to help you derive medicine rather than memorise it. Understand how the body normally works → disturb the system → predict the consequences → recognise them in a patient → use that reasoning to make sense of the clinical problem.

Guided interactive lessons combine audio guidance, visual mechanisms, questions and clinical application — so you actively predict and reason as the lesson unfolds.
