Physiology is the foundation. Pathophysiology is the bridge. Clinical reasoning brings it back to the patient. MeducateMe is built so you can move between all three rather than memorising each in isolation.

Guided interactive lessons combine visual mechanisms, questions, clinical application and audio guidance so you actively predict and reason as the lesson unfolds.
