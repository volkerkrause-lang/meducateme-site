04 · Potassium

THE NUMBER CAN MISLEAD
THE BODY IS DEPLETED

DKA can present with a normal or raised serum potassium even though total-body potassium is depleted. Insulin deficiency, acidaemia and hyperosmolality favour extracellular potassium, while osmotic diuresis causes major urinary losses.
