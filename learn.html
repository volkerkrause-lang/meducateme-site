CLINICAL INTERRUPTION

A 12-year-old has glucose 28 mmol/L and serum K⁺ 4.8 mmol/L. Is total-body potassium normal?

Use the physiology, not the serum number alone.

Contextual flashcard: why can serum potassium look reassuring in DKA?
Acidosis and insulin deficiency shift potassium out of cells, while osmotic diuresis causes major urinary potassium loss. Serum concentration can therefore mask total-body depletion.

EXPLAIN IT BACK

One signal. Two pathways.

Loss of insulin creates a fasting signal in a glucose-rich body: glucose accumulates outside cells while fat is mobilised into ketones. Treatment works by reversing those physiological consequences.
