The MeducateMe philosophy

UNDERSTAND THE NORMAL
THEN UNDERSTAND THE CHANGE

Medicine becomes easier to remember when facts are connected. Start with normal physiology, understand what changes in disease, and use those connections to reason through the patient.

If you understand why something happens, there is much less to memorise.

PHYSIOLOGY→PATHOPHYSIOLOGY→PATIENT
Learn by following cause and consequence

NORMAL → CHANGE → EFFECT
THEN APPLY IT

Start with how a system should work. Change one part. Predict what follows — through pathways, cells and organs to symptoms, signs, investigations and treatment.
